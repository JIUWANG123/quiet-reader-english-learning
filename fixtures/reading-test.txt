Chapter 1 — The Letter

She found herself strangely reluctant to leave. The station was empty, and she still had not delivered the letter.

“I couldn't bring myself to tell her,” Anna said. Her sister loved this station, and the letter announced that it would close next month.

The stationmaster noticed that the envelope was already open. He regarded her with suspicion. Only Anna had been in the office that morning.

“You knew,” he said quietly. The date on the letter was a week old. Anna had kept the news to herself.

Chapter 2 — A Small Beginning

The children gave Anna a paper flower. She had given them a little help with their garden, and they wanted to thank her. She was taking the flower home.

“Will you give it to your sister?” a child asked. Anna smiled, showing her teeth.

A man made a contemptuous remark about the station. Anna did not answer. She was no longer reluctant to speak to her sister.
